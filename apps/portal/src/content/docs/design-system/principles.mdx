The ideas that guide every decision in the Harness Design System.

## Cohesion

Every experience should feel connected, with no dead ends or isolated surfaces.

## Consistency

The same patterns should behave the same way everywhere, so users can predict what comes next.

## Sweat the details

Every detail is considered, creating an experience that feels thoughtful even when users can't explain why.

## Accessibility

Accessibility is built in from the start, with AA as the baseline—not something added later.

## Modularity

Components and patterns are built to evolve, extend, and adapt without breaking the larger system.

## Authenticity

Harness should feel distinct, not generic. We use familiar conventions where they help, but bring a clear point of view that feels unmistakably ours.
