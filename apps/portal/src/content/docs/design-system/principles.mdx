The ideas that guide every decision in the Harness Design System.

<div className="cn-principles-grid not-content">
  <div className="cn-principle-card">
    <h3 className="cn-principle-title">Cohesion</h3>
    <p className="cn-principle-body">Every experience should feel connected, with no dead ends or isolated surfaces.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="cn-principle-card">
    <h3 className="cn-principle-title">Consistency</h3>
    <p className="cn-principle-body">The same patterns should behave the same way everywhere, so users can predict what comes next.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="cn-principle-card">
    <h3 className="cn-principle-title">Sweat the details</h3>
    <p className="cn-principle-body">Every detail is considered, creating an experience that feels thoughtful even when users can't explain why.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="cn-principle-card">
    <h3 className="cn-principle-title">Accessibility</h3>
    <p className="cn-principle-body">Accessibility is built in from the start, with AA as the baseline—not something added later.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="cn-principle-card">
    <h3 className="cn-principle-title">Modularity</h3>
    <p className="cn-principle-body">Components and patterns are built to evolve, extend, and adapt without breaking the larger system.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="cn-principle-card">
    <h3 className="cn-principle-title">Authenticity</h3>
    <p className="cn-principle-body">Harness should feel distinct, not generic. We use familiar conventions where they help, but bring a clear point of view that feels unmistakably ours.</p>
  </div>
</div>
