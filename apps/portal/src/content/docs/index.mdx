## Principles

<div className="cn-principles-grid not-content">
  <div className="cn-principle-card">
    <h3 className="cn-principle-title">Cohesion</h3>
    <p className="cn-principle-body">One experience connects seamlessly to the next without dead ends. No surface on the platform should feel as if it were designed in isolation.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="cn-principle-card">
    <h3 className="cn-principle-title">Consistency</h3>
    <p className="cn-principle-body">The same pattern means the same thing everywhere. A user who learns one part of the platform can easily and intuitively predict how the rest behaves.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="cn-principle-card">
    <h3 className="cn-principle-title">Sweat the details</h3>
    <p className="cn-principle-body">Every detail is considered. Users feel the thoughtfulness in the UI even when they can't name why.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="cn-principle-card">
    <h3 className="cn-principle-title">Accessibility</h3>
    <p className="cn-principle-body">Built AA accessible from the ground up, not retrofitted. Accessibility is a default, not a checklist item at the end.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="cn-principle-card">
    <h3 className="cn-principle-title">Modularity</h3>
    <p className="cn-principle-body">The system is built to evolve. Components and patterns can be extended or swapped without breaking the whole, so the system keeps pace as the product and our workflows change.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="cn-principle-card">
    <h3 className="cn-principle-title">Authenticity</h3>
    <p className="cn-principle-body">The system feels genuinely Harness Brand, not borrowed from a generic AI pattern library. We use familiar conventions where they serve the user, but with a distinct point of view so Harness stands out among competitors. We evoke emotions.</p>
  </div>
</div>
