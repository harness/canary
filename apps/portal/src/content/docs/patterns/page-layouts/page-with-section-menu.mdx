A vertical menu of grouped links on the left and the selected section on the right, with an optional back link above the menu. Use it for areas with several related sections, such as repository or webhook settings. In product code this is `ContentLayoutWithSidebar`.

## Example

A wireframe of a repository settings page inside the full application shell, built from the same components production uses.

The app sidebar and the collapsed AI chat rail are boilerplate, rebuilt from the production shell and shown disabled for context only.

:::note
These demos are drawn on a 1440 × 900 screen and scaled down to fit the page, so they appear smaller than 100%. Sizes, spacing and text in the demos are reduced by the same percentage; the real values are listed in the Usage tab.
:::

## Anatomy

1. **Page header.** Breadcrumb, page title and a short description, as in the [standard page](/patterns/page-layouts/standard-page/).
2. **Back link (optional).** Sits above the menu and returns to the parent list. Use it when the area was opened from a list, such as a single webhook.
3. **Section menu.** A fixed 228px column of links. The active link is bold with a hover-colored background. Group related links and divide groups with a separator, with an optional small group title.
4. **Section content.** Fills the remaining width. Each section starts with a subsection heading, and sections are divided by separators.

## Guidelines

- Use the section menu for **four or more** related sections. For two or three, use tabs in the page header.
- The menu stays in view while the section content scrolls.
- The section content has a **maximum width** (640px here), so forms stay readable. Empty space to the right of it is intentional; do not stretch fields to fill the page.
- Keep link labels short enough to stay on one line; they are truncated, not wrapped.
- Keep destructive actions in their own section at the bottom, separated from regular settings.
