---
title: Steppers
description: Guided multi-step flows, from page-level setup wizards to single and dual pane onboarding steppers.
---

Steppers guide people through a task one step at a time, showing progress and keeping the current step in focus. Pick the variant by how much room the flow needs and whether it is a setup wizard or a guided, conversational flow.

- [Stepped Flow](/patterns/page-layouts/stepped-flow/): a full page with a step rail beside the current step's content and a footer for Back and Continue. Use it for setup and creation wizards with several form-heavy steps.
- [Single Pane](/growth-patterns/single-pane-stepper/): a single column with a stepper timeline and an inline stack of cards. Use it for short guided flows.
- [Single Pane Chat](/growth-patterns/single-pane-stepper-v2/): the same single column, with completed answers shown as chat messages instead of accordion cards.
- Dual Pane: a full-page flow with resizable panes and a card-based decision tree. Not yet available.

The Single Pane and Dual Pane steppers are growth patterns, for onboarding and activation flows only. Do not use them for general product UI.
